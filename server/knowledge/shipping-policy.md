# VERANO Shipping Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy applies to every order placed on VERANO.

## Where does VERANO deliver?

VERANO currently delivers within India only. When you add a delivery address, you need a valid 6-digit PIN code. We do not ship outside India at the moment.

## How much does shipping cost?

- If your order subtotal is below ₹999, a flat shipping fee of ₹99 is added.
- If your order subtotal is ₹999 or more, shipping is free.
- The subtotal is the total price of the items in your order, before shipping.
- Cash on delivery has no extra charge. You pay only the order total.

Example 1: An order with a subtotal of ₹799 pays ₹99 shipping, so the total is ₹898.
Example 2: An order with a subtotal of ₹1,499 gets free shipping, so the total is ₹1,499.

The shipping fee is shown at checkout before you pay.

## How long does it take to process an order?

After you place an order, VERANO takes 1 to 2 business days to confirm it, pack it and hand it over for delivery. Online orders are processed only after the payment is received.

## How long does delivery take?

Delivery time is counted from the day your order is shipped, not the day you placed it.

| Delivery area | Estimated delivery time |
|---|---|
| Metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad) | 3 to 5 business days |
| Other cities and towns | 5 to 7 business days |
| Remote areas | 7 to 10 business days |

Business days do not include Sundays and public holidays. These are estimates, and delays can happen because of weather, festivals or courier issues.

## How can I track my order?

Open your orders in your VERANO account to see the current status of each order. An order moves through these statuses:

- **Pending payment**: you chose online payment and VERANO is waiting for the payment. The items are held for you for 15 minutes.
- **Placed**: your order was received (and paid, for online orders).
- **Confirmed**: VERANO has accepted the order and is preparing it.
- **Shipped**: your order has left VERANO and is on its way.
- **Delivered**: your order has reached you.
- **Cancelled**: the order was cancelled.

## What payment methods are available?

VERANO offers two payment methods:

1. **Cash on delivery (COD)**: pay in cash when the order arrives.
2. **Online payment through Razorpay**: pay with UPI, cards, net banking or wallets at checkout.

## What happens if I do not complete an online payment?

When you choose online payment, VERANO holds your items for 15 minutes so nobody else buys them. If the payment is not completed in 15 minutes, the order is cancelled automatically and the items go back into stock. If the payment window is still open, you can return to your order and pay again. If it has expired, you need to place the order again.

## Is there a limit on how many items I can order?

You can order up to 10 units of the same product in the same size and colour in one order. You can also only order what is in stock. If a size or colour is out of stock, it cannot be added to your cart.

## Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?

No. The delivery address is saved with your order when you place it and cannot be changed afterwards. If you made a mistake, cancel the order before it is shipped and place a new one with the correct address. See the cancellation policy for how to cancel.

## What happens if I am not available at delivery?

The delivery partner makes up to 3 delivery attempts. If all attempts fail, the order is returned to VERANO. For online payments, the amount is refunded to your original payment method. For cash on delivery, you have not paid anything, so there is nothing to refund.

## What should I do if my order arrives damaged?

Contact VERANO support within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos of the item and the packaging. See the returns and exchanges policy for what happens next.
