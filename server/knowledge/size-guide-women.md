# VERANO Women's Size Guide

Last updated: November 2026

This guide applies to women's clothing on VERANO: kurtas, kurta sets, tops, dresses, jeans, trousers, jackets, sleepwear and officewear. VERANO women's sizes are S, M, L, XL and XXL. Sarees and some accessories come in Free Size.

## How do I measure myself?

Use a soft measuring tape and measure over light clothing or underwear.

- **Bust**: measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level.
- **Waist**: measure around the narrowest part of your waist, usually just above the belly button.
- **Hip**: measure around the fullest part of your hips.

Keep the tape snug but not tight. Compare your measurements with the chart below. Measurements in the chart are body measurements, not the measurements of the garment.

- Size S fits bust 34 inches (86 cm), waist 28 inches (71 cm) and hip 36 inches (91 cm).
- Size M fits bust 36 inches (91 cm), waist 30 inches (76 cm) and hip 38 inches (97 cm).
- Size L fits bust 38 inches (97 cm), waist 32 inches (81 cm) and hip 40 inches (102 cm).

- Size XXL fits bust 42 inches (107 cm), waist 36 inches (91 cm) and hip 44 inches (112 cm).

## Which size should I choose for kurtas, kurta sets, tops and dresses?

Choose your size by your **bust** measurement first. If your hip measurement falls in a larger size than your bust, choose the larger size, so the garment sits comfortably over your hips. For a kurta set, the size chart applies to the kurta. Trousers in a set follow your waist and hip measurements.

## Which size should I choose for jeans and trousers?

Choose your size by your **waist** measurement, and check your hip measurement too. If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one for a comfortable fit.

## What do the fit types mean?

Some product descriptions mention a fit. Here is what each one means:

- **Regular fit**: a standard, comfortable fit with a little room. Choose your usual size.
- **Slim fit**: closer to the body. If you are between sizes, or prefer more room, choose the next size up.
- **Relaxed fit**: looser and more comfortable. Choose your usual size for a relaxed look, or one size down for a closer fit.
- **Oversized fit**: intentionally roomy. Choose your usual size for the intended look, or one size down for less volume.

## What if I am between two sizes?

- For regular and slim fits, choose the larger size.
- For relaxed and oversized fits, choose the smaller size if you want a closer fit.
- If you plan to wear layers underneath, choose the larger size.

## What is Free Size?

Free Size items, such as sarees, are not made to a body measurement and are meant to fit most people. Check the product description for details of what is included.

## What if the size does not fit?

If the size does not fit, you can ask for an exchange within 7 days of delivery, subject to stock. See the returns and exchanges policy. If the order has not shipped yet, you can cancel it and order a different size.

Email support@verano.example with the product name and your bust, waist and hip measurements, and VERANO support will suggest a size.
