# VERANO Men's Size Guide

Last updated: November 2026

This guide applies to men's clothing on VERANO: shirts, t-shirts, jeans, trousers, jackets, sleepwear and officewear. VERANO men's sizes are S, M, L, XL and XXL. Some accessories come in Free Size.

## How do I measure myself?

Use a soft measuring tape and measure over light clothing or underwear.

- **Chest**: measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level under your arms and across your shoulder blades.
- **Waist**: measure around your natural waistline, usually just above the belly button. For jeans and trousers, measure where you normally wear them.

Keep the tape snug but not tight. Compare your measurements with the chart below. Measurements in the chart are body measurements, not the measurements of the garment.

- Size S fits chest 36 inches (91 cm) and waist 30 inches (76 cm).
- Size M fits chest 38 inches (97 cm) and waist 32 inches (81 cm).
- Size L fits chest 40 inches (102 cm) and waist 34 inches (86 cm).
- Size XL fits chest 42 inches (107 cm) and waist 36 inches (91 cm).
- Size XXL fits chest 44 inches (112 cm) and waist 38 inches (97 cm).

## Which size should I choose for shirts, t-shirts and jackets?

Choose your size by your **chest** measurement. For jackets, choose the size that lets you wear a shirt or a light sweater underneath. If your chest falls between two sizes, choose the larger size for shirts and jackets.

## Which size should I choose for jeans and trousers?

Choose your size by your **waist** measurement. VERANO jeans and trousers use letter sizes, which match these waist sizes:

| VERANO size | Approximate waist size (inches) |
|---|---|
| S | 30 |
| M | 32 |
| L | 34 |
| XL | 36 |
| XXL | 38 |

If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one for a comfortable fit.

## Which size should I choose for sleepwear and officewear?

For sleepwear, choose your usual size for a comfortable fit, or one size up if you prefer a looser fit for sleeping. For officewear such as formal shirts and trousers, choose your size by chest (for shirts) and waist (for trousers), and avoid sizing down, because formal clothes should not feel tight when you sit or move.

## What do the fit types mean?

Some product descriptions mention a fit. Here is what each one means:

- **Regular fit**: a standard, comfortable fit with a little room. Choose your usual size.
- **Slim fit**: closer to the body. If you are between sizes, or prefer more room, choose the next size up.
- **Relaxed fit**: looser and more comfortable. Choose your usual size for a relaxed look, or one size down for a closer fit.
- **Oversized fit**: intentionally roomy. Choose your usual size for the intended look, or one size down for less volume.

## What if I am between two sizes?

- For regular and slim fits, choose the larger size.
- For relaxed and oversized fits, choose the smaller size if you want a closer fit.
- If you plan to wear layers underneath, choose the larger size.

## What if the size does not fit?

If the size does not fit, you can ask for an exchange within 7 days of delivery, subject to stock. See the returns and exchanges policy. If the order has not shipped yet, you can cancel it and order a different size.

## Where can I get help choosing a size?

Email support@verano.example with the product name and your chest and waist measurements, and VERANO support will suggest a size.
