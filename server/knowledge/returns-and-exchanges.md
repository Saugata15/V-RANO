# VERANO Returns and Exchanges Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy covers items that have already been delivered. To stop an order before it ships, see the cancellation and refund policy.

## What is the return window?

You can request a return or exchange within 7 days of the delivery date. Requests made after 7 days cannot be accepted.

## How do I request a return or exchange?

Returns and exchanges are handled by VERANO support. There is no automatic return button yet.

1. Email support@verano.example within 7 days of delivery.
2. Include your order number (for example VRN-20261124-AB12CD), the item you want to return, and the reason.
3. For damaged, defective or wrong items, attach clear photos of the item and the packaging.
4. VERANO reviews the request and replies with the next steps, usually within 2 business days.
5. If approved, VERANO arranges the pickup. Pickup is free of charge.

## Which items can be returned?

An item can be returned if all of these are true:

- It is unused, unwashed and unaltered.
- The original tags are attached.
- It is in its original packaging.
- The request is made within 7 days of delivery.

## Which items cannot be returned?

- Items that have been worn, washed, altered or damaged after delivery.
- Items without the original tags or packaging.
- Requests made after the 7-day window.

## What if I received a damaged, defective or wrong item?

Contact support within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos. VERANO will either send a replacement (if it is in stock) or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer. You do not pay for the return pickup.

## Can I exchange an item for a different size?

Yes, if the size you want is in stock. Email support with your order number, the item, and the size you want. If the new size is not available, you can choose a refund instead. Exchanges follow the same 7-day window and the same condition rules as returns.

If you are unsure about your size, check the women's or men's size guide before ordering. It lists measurements for every size.

## How will I get my refund?

Once the returned item reaches VERANO and passes a quality check, the refund is processed within 5 to 7 business days.

- **Paid online (Razorpay)**: the refund goes back to the same payment method you used.
- **Cash on delivery**: you did not pay online, so VERANO refunds you by bank transfer or UPI. Support will ask for the details when your return is approved.

The refund covers the price of the returned items. The original shipping fee is refunded only for damaged, defective or wrong items.

## Can I return only part of an order?

Yes. You can return one or more items from an order. Items you keep are not affected.

## Why was my return request rejected?

A return can be rejected if the item shows signs of use, washing or alteration, if tags or packaging are missing, or if the request was made after 7 days. Support will explain the reason in the reply.

## How can I contact VERANO about returns?

Email support@verano.example with your order number. Support replies within 2 business days.
