# VERANO Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains when you can cancel a VERANO order and how refunds work. For sending back an item after delivery, see the returns and exchanges policy.

## Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel an order yourself as long as it has not been shipped. That means you can cancel when the order status is:

- Pending payment
- Placed
- Confirmed

You cannot cancel an order once its status is **Shipped** or **Delivered**. If the order has already shipped, wait for it to arrive and then follow the returns and exchanges policy. An order that is already **Cancelled** cannot be cancelled again.

## How do I cancel an order?

1. Open your orders in your VERANO account.
2. Select the order you want to cancel.
3. Choose cancel, and optionally give a reason.

The order is cancelled straight away, and the items go back into stock.

## Is there a fee for cancelling?

No. VERANO does not charge a cancellation fee.

## Will I get a refund if I cancel a cash on delivery order?

For cash on delivery (COD) orders, you pay only when the order is delivered. If you cancel before it ships, you have not paid anything, so there is nothing to refund.

## Will I get a refund if I cancel an online payment order?

Yes. If you paid online through Razorpay and the order is cancelled, the full amount you paid, including the shipping fee, is refunded to the same payment method you used (the same card, UPI account, bank account or wallet).

Refunds usually reach your account in 5 to 7 business days after VERANO starts the refund. The exact time depends on your bank or payment provider.

## What does the payment status mean on a cancelled order?

- **Refund pending**: VERANO has started the refund and it is being processed.
- **Refunded**: the refund has been sent back to your original payment method.

If the status stays on refund pending for more than 7 business days, contact support with your order number.

## What if my online order was cancelled because I did not pay in time?

When you choose online payment, the order is held for 15 minutes. If the payment is not completed in that time, the order is cancelled automatically and the items are released. No money is taken, because the payment did not happen. You can place the order again.

## What if I paid but the order was already cancelled?

Sometimes a payment completes at the very moment the 15-minute window closes. If money was taken but the order had already been cancelled, VERANO refunds the full amount automatically to your original payment method. You do not need to ask for it. If you do not see the refund within 7 business days, contact support.

## Can VERANO cancel my order?

In rare cases VERANO may cancel an order, for example when an item cannot be supplied. If a paid online order is cancelled by VERANO, you receive a full refund to your original payment method.

## Can I change the size, colour or address instead of cancelling?

No. An order cannot be edited after it is placed. If you need a different size, colour or address, cancel the order before it ships and place a new one. Cancelling is free.

## How can I contact VERANO about a cancellation or refund?

Email support@verano.example with your order number (for example VRN-20261124-AB12CD) and a short description of the problem.
