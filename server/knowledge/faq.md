# VERANO Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: November 2026

## About VERANO

### What is VERANO?

VERANO is an online fashion store for men and women. You can shop clothing such as shirts, t-shirts, jeans, trousers, jackets, kurtas, dresses, tops, sarees, sleepwear, officewear and activewear, plus footwear and accessories.

### Where does VERANO deliver?

VERANO delivers within India only. You need a valid 6-digit PIN code for your delivery address.

### How can I contact VERANO?

Email support@verano.example and include your order number if your question is about an order. Support replies within 2 business days.

## Account

### Do I need an account to place an order?

Yes. You need to be logged in to add items to your cart and place an order.

### How do I create an account or log in?

You can register with your email and a password, or continue with your Google account. If you registered with email, you can also link your Google account later from your account security settings.

### How do I change my password?

Log in and use the change password option in your account. You will need your current password and a new password.

### Can I change my profile picture?

Yes. You can upload a new profile picture from your account. Images must be JPEG, JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF and up to 1 MB.

## Products and sizes

### How do I find my size?

Check the size guide. VERANO has a women's size guide and a men's size guide with body measurements for sizes S, M, L, XL and XXL. Each size lists chest or bust, waist and hip measurements in inches and centimetres.

### What does Free Size mean?

Free Size items, such as sarees, are not made to a body measurement and are meant to fit most people.

### Why can't I select a particular size or colour?

That size and colour combination is out of stock or is not offered for the product. Each product lists the sizes and colours that are available.

### What do slim, regular, relaxed and oversized fit mean?

Regular is a standard comfortable fit, slim is closer to the body, relaxed is looser, and oversized is intentionally roomy. The size guide explains which size to choose for each fit.

### Is the price shown the final price?

The price on the product page is the selling price. Some products also show a higher crossed-out price, which is the original price before the discount. At checkout, shipping is added if your order subtotal is below ₹999.

## Cart and checkout

### How many items can I add to my cart?

You can add up to 10 units of the same product in the same size and colour. You can only add as many as are in stock.

### Why is an item in my cart marked unavailable?

An item is marked unavailable when the product is no longer sold, the size or colour is no longer offered, it is out of stock, or there is less stock than the quantity you chose. Remove it or reduce the quantity before you check out.

### Do the prices in my cart change?

Yes, they can. Your cart always shows the current price of each product. The price is fixed when you place the order, and the order keeps the price you paid.

### Can I save more than one delivery address?

Yes. You can save several addresses and choose one at checkout. You can also set one as your default address.

### Does VERANO have coupon codes?

Not at the moment.

## Payments

### What payment methods does VERANO accept?

You can pay by cash on delivery, or online through Razorpay using UPI, cards, net banking or wallets.

### Is there an extra charge for cash on delivery?

No. Cash on delivery has no extra fee.

### How much is shipping?

Shipping is ₹99 for orders with a subtotal below ₹999. Orders of ₹999 or more get free shipping.

### My payment went through, but my order says pending payment. What should I do?

Wait a few minutes and refresh your orders page. VERANO confirms the payment automatically, even if you closed the page right after paying. If the order is still pending after 15 minutes and money was deducted, contact support with your order number. If the order expired, your money is refunded automatically.

### Why was my online order cancelled automatically?

When you choose online payment, VERANO holds your items for 15 minutes. If the payment is not completed in that time, the order is cancelled and the items go back into stock. No money is taken in that case. You can place the order again.

### Is my card information stored by VERANO?

No. Online payments are handled by Razorpay. VERANO does not see or store your card details.

## Orders and delivery

### What do the order statuses mean?

- **Pending payment**: waiting for your online payment.
- **Placed**: the order was received.
- **Confirmed**: VERANO accepted the order and is preparing it.
- **Shipped**: the order is on its way to you.
- **Delivered**: the order reached you.
- **Cancelled**: the order was cancelled.

### How can I track my order?

Open your orders in your account to see the status of each order. Your order number looks like VRN-20261124-AB12CD.

### How long does delivery take?

Delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days in metro cities, 5 to 7 business days in other cities and towns, and 7 to 10 business days in remote areas, counted from the day the order ships. See the shipping policy for details.

### Can I change my address, size or colour after placing an order?

No. An order cannot be edited after it is placed. If you need to change something, cancel the order before it ships and place a new one. Cancelling is free.

## Cancellations, returns and refunds

### Can I cancel my order?

Yes, until it is shipped. You can cancel when the status is pending payment, placed or confirmed. You cannot cancel once it is shipped or delivered.

### Will I get a refund if I cancel?

If you paid online, the full amount is refunded to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 7 business days. If you chose cash on delivery, you have not paid yet, so there is nothing to refund.

### Can I return an item?

Yes, within 7 days of delivery. The item must be unused, unwashed and unaltered, with the original tags and packaging. Email support@verano.example with your order number to start a return.

### Can I exchange an item for a different size?

Yes, within 7 days of delivery and subject to stock. If the size is not available, you can choose a refund instead.

### What if I receive a damaged or wrong item?

Contact support within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos. VERANO will send a replacement or give you a full refund, and you do not pay for the return pickup.

### How long do refunds take?

Refunds to your original payment method usually take 5 to 7 business days. The exact time depends on your bank or payment provider.
